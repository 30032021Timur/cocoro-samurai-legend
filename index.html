How to fight

  • Move WASD or the stick. Jump Space or JUMP. A second press is a double jump.
  • J / SLASH chains jab, kick, katana, spinning slash. SHUH! marks the blade.
  • K / BLOCK chip-damages frontal hits. Tap block as the strike lands for COUNTER!
  • L / DODGE rolls through attacks. U / KI spends ki on Thunder Strike.
  • OTD grunts walk in and claw. Ninjas dash and throw stars. Blades use a katana and can guard.
  • Boss levels: Kiba (1), Goro (5), Take (10), Kage (15), Homa (20), Yuki (25), Neon (30). Three phases. At zero health: FINISH HIM! then J, J, U (or FATALITY three times).
  • Kiba, Kage, and Yuki use blue flame. Goro, Take, Homa, and Neon use thunder. Armor breaks into metal. The demon becomes dark energy. No gore. The other levels are fights without a fatality.
